THE IMPORTANT DETAILS

Privacy, in plain words.

Updated 4 October 2026

When you prepare a visit request, you can provide your name, mobile number and/or email, service address, the kind of help you need, your preferred time and an optional description of the issue. We use those details to understand your request and reply about service availability and pricing.

When information is sent

Choosing Prepare my email request opens your email app with the details you entered addressed to AnyService.support@gmail.com. The message is sent only if you choose Send in that app. Your email provider and AnyService then handle the email so we can respond to you and discuss the request.

What you can choose

You can leave the form without sending an email. Please share a mobile number or email only if you want AnyService to be able to reply. Do not include payment-card details, passwords or other information that is not needed to describe a home repair.

Questions or changes

To ask about a request, correct information you have emailed, or ask us to remove a message, write to AnyService.support@gmail.com. Email providers may retain messages under their own policies.

Website and hosting

The website does not use advertising or analytics scripts. It is hosted as a static site; the hosting provider may process technical connection data under its own terms. Your form entries are not sent to the website just by typing them.
