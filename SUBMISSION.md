# AnyService — submission notes

## Brand and concept

AnyService is a home-repair company built around a simple belief: good work should begin close to home. It connects people with local electricians, plumbers, carpenters and appliance-care service providers for the everyday fixes that make a home feel like itself again. Instead of treating repair as a list of transactions, AnyService makes it a neighbourly exchange—with a clear place to start, a helpful human at the other end, and skilled hands that know the work. The identity pairs a deep, steady blue with repair-shop lime and warm coral. A welcoming launch sequence, playful original illustrations and a small visit picker turn the first step into part of the brand.

## Technology

HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript. The site is static, responsive and runs directly from the local folder. There are no packages or runtime network requests.

## Assets and credits

The logo, service illustrations, house artwork and small icons were created specifically for this prototype, with AI-assisted design and implementation. Typography uses local system fonts. No third-party imagery, fonts, icon packs or JavaScript libraries are used. See [ASSETS.md](ASSETS.md) for the asset list.

## Offline presentation

Open `index.html` from this folder, then select **Press to step inside**. For the profile flow, select **Book a visit** or open `account.html`. The profile and sign-in forms are front-end demonstrations: they do not send or store personal details and do not connect to authentication or code delivery.
